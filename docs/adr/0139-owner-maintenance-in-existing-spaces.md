# 139. Owner maintenance in existing spaces

Status: accepted, 2026-09-30. Uses the shared flow of [ADR 0136](0136-system-state-view-and-shared-maintenance-flow.md) for the owners of [ADR 0135](0135-owner-state-maintenance.md).

## Decision

Each owner's shipped maintenance appears where that owner's records already live. Every control is local-only and follows the live WebSocket selection. Each control calls only that owner's named operations.

- **Signal**: clears all captured content as one scope, gated on processing being paused. The plan also waits for reads and inference to drain. Its correlated Infer request IDs form a separate, explicit Infer selection. Clearing Signal never cascades into Infer. A separate Maintenance disclosure rebaselines checkpoints for all current sources (not future sources), after the first baseline, pause and pending/active interpretation drain. Resume skips current upstream messages; captured evidence, suppression and Infer outcomes remain. Status shows the checkpoint generation and latest per-source resets. This is not historical replay or transcript erase.
- **Lab Infer**: selects up to 100 terminal requests; running and already-cleared requests cannot be selected.
- **Inbox**: in the dismissed view, selects dismissed notifications that still hold content. Clearing never answers anything, and the original outcome stays.
- **Content**: a Storage window lists upload stages, each retired at its exact revision. It also lists collection blobs one digest prefix at a time with their item and stage references. Only unreferenced blobs can be selected for a collection plan. Temporary publications pages exact claims, with nullable bytes and per-row liveness/incarnation blockers; its collapsed Maintenance disclosure collects at most 100 unblocked claim UUIDs through receipt-only recovery. Published objects, source references, permanent claim/receipt evidence, legacy/unattributed paths and quarantine remain. Document Editor and Storage expose collapsed, read-only Retained history disclosures; Storage accepts an exact slug for removed documents. Revision-paged path/commit/blob metadata includes commits scanned, current/historical paths, remote presence and retention caveats. Inspection errors are coverage failures, never empty-history claims. No apply, Git rewrite, device reset or external erasure is offered. See [ADR 0156](0156-content-retention-and-publication-maintenance.md).
- **Proc**: a terminal run can clear its output, and a finished execution can clear its captured content. Each is one exact record. A completed output clear remounts the run's log. A removed schedule's detail ends in a Maintenance disclosure that redacts its stored definition (action input, or process arguments, environment and working directory) and then shows when, with the retained spec digest. Active and Brain-protected schedules are refused by the plan; captured executions are separate selections.
- **Fleet queue**: a Bot's Queue view lists each admission's original size, digest, generation and cleared marker. Its Maintenance disclosure, which also reveals the row checkboxes, clears queued message bodies for exact terminal entries (at most 100) or for one retired generation of this incarnation. An unknown admission stays unknown and is never a retry; entries recorded without a generation can only be selected by ID.
- **Lab Infer catalog**: a Maintenance disclosure evicts model observations from the server's memory for the chosen account or for all accounts. It is a direct action with an inline two-step confirm, not a plan: the catalog is derived and keeps no receipt. Clearing never discovers anything, and once the catalog was cleared in the Lab, choosing an account no longer discovers its models either; Discover models is the only way back.
- **Accounts Usage**: clears exact account/scope pairs and, separately, the Grok Bot observation. Provider quota, billing and credentials are untouched.
- **HUD**: a retired-root Chat focus can be removed from the item's focus list. The Resources window also lists every retired-root focus, including saved "no focus" rows that no item shows. An item's detail ends in a collapsed Maintenance disclosure that clears stored text, either journal bodies or the item and journal as a permanent tombstone, for the item alone or for its whole subtree (at most 100 items in one plan). The scope is chosen explicitly; open Worker admissions and live Chat focus block the plan, which the shared review lists. Identity, hierarchy, state and dependency IDs stay, and nothing here completes, cancels or reopens work. The item, tree and timeline show "Content cleared" in place of cleared text; a tombstone is read-only, and the timeline re-reads from the start when the item's content generation advances.
- **Workers**: a read-only Files tab shows the retained worktree. Closed Workers can prepare an exact worktree reset in Changes, preserving the old tip at `refs/stack/retained/<worker>` while losing uncommitted files/index state. Session maintenance offers transcript-only logical redaction, native-session purge with all observed and owner-proved preconditions and the exact disclosed native IDs, branch collection with deliberate per-branch unmerged consent, and account-shared derived model-catalog clearing (also available for open Workers). The Workers list pages recorded branch claims after the Closed group, independently of Worker filters or record removal; collected claims remain retired and cannot be adopted. Plans show blockers and independent retained copies. Unknown/partial receipts and uncertain admissions stay in receipt-only recovery across reload: no resend, new plan or close-and-rearm control is offered. Conversation/Records discard cached bodies on `contentClearedAt`, and Turns distinguish cleared prompts. Close, remove, permission answers and other lifecycle controls remain separate; maintenance never resumes, starts, cancels or closes a Worker. See [ADR 0153](0153-exact-worker-state-maintenance.md).
- **Roles**: the existing Shims window lists paged launch directories with live, retained or unknown state, issue and modification time. Manual refresh observes native/file changes. Its Maintenance disclosure selects at most 100 retained directories; live and missing-lock/unknown rows block clearing. External history and credentials and Bot/Worker materializations stay; no native process is stopped.
- **Managed settings**: each Bot, Worker or future-instance defaults editor has an Old receipts disclosure. One exact target and a retention window of at least seven days select receipts below its current saved revision. Current, young and unknown-age receipts and minimal replay tombstones remain; settings and native application never change. This makes no disk-reclamation or secret-erasure claim.
- **Accounts Auth**: the Worker-account inspector has a model-cache Maintenance disclosure with disabled-account, idle-sign-in and runtime observations. Only Codex/OpenCode models.json is supported; Devin/Claude are unsupported. The owner plan verifies drained runtime/catalog/teardown and safe paths. Disable/drain remain separate; credentials, keychains, native sessions, sibling accounts and other files stay.
- **System Access**: Expired history groups exact UI sessions, pairings and invitations into independent maintenance selections. Active/unexpired authority is excluded; revocation stays separate. Identity, credentials, revocations and enrollment/Share replay evidence remain, as do device/browser copies. Recent audit discloses automatic-only retention: cleanup bounds existing sequence rows before appending new entries, so observed rows may exceed 1,000. There is no manual prune control.
- **Xcom**: lives in System, since it has no space of its own. The window shows status and a persistent pause. Because Xcom publishes no events, a paused sync is re-read every two seconds for at most a minute until it drains. Post removal requires explicit reimport and orphan-author choices; checkpoint reset states that scanning resumes and may spend again.
- **System State**: each owner links to the space holding its existing controls. The owner groups state the maintained backend gaps as unsupported and offer no controls for them.
- **Brain**: Job details clear captured payloads for exact terminal orphan jobs; active claims, indexed documents and immutable operator jobs block via the plan. The Jobs Run filter offers payload clearing for that exact terminal/drained Run and all its captured jobs, retaining immutable recovery authorization and generation/snapshot authority. Cleared payload markers disable Retry/Reveal. Each Source has paused/drained removal and checkpoint-reset disclosures; the protected Proc schedule is checked by the owner. Reset stays paused and admits nothing, while later explicit resume/sync may re-read, re-admit and spend. Retired identities show Retired and never offer Resume/sync/manifest actions. Prior definitions/checkpoint history, documents, child jobs and independent Artifact/backup copies remain. Stranded-Artifact collection stays API-only because no existing surface lists exact digests.
- **Scrape**: Queue row Maintenance disclosures offer cancel for pending generations, retry for failed generations and discard for failed or receipt-retired remaining files. Rows show request/action/receipt status fences. Exact native PID/token claims and publication recovery block via the plan; maintenance never breaks claim evidence or targets external destinations. Retry publishes a new pending generation which may later extract, spend or publish; admission is not extraction completion. Checks' Corpus replay section lists final local sample-NNN captures only for one selected preset, with at most 100 exact captures per clear plan. Selection and preset freeze while active. Shipped fixtures/presets/canaries, temporary publication, Browse sessions, independent copies and unattributed retirement quarantine remain separate. Queue and corpus effects use receipt-only recovery after uncertainty across reload, retaining the original UUID and planned retry filenames without automatic resend/rearm.
- **Browse**: Profile menus host reset and exact-origin cookies/storage/CacheStorage dialogs (never scoped HTTP cache or history). Reset retains ID/default/assignment and advances generation while losing old volume data. Plans enforce stopped verified Bot, closed selected/uncertain controllers, resolved handoffs and exact provider ownership; site-data planning requires ready CDP and never starts/navigates. A durable profile fence shows its receipt and exact remaining/uncertain resources before explicit generation-bound release, which only acknowledges inspection and never completes or reruns effects. History selects resolved handoff bodies for logical redaction and shows cleared markers while retaining identity, outcome, timing and replay digests. Toolchain's Hypeman section pages verified owned volumes; referenced/mounted volumes are not selectable, unavailable provider is an error and bytes are unmeasured. Reset, site data and volume collection use receipt-only recovery across reload. No implicit lifecycle action, session closure or VM launch; native writers and independent copies/backups remain outside scope. See [ADR 0155](0155-exact-browser-provider-maintenance.md).

A completed receipt empties the selection it applied to. A partial or unknown receipt keeps the selection for inspection.

Maintenance 01 (HUD, Fleet queue, Proc schedules, Lab catalog) sets the shape later slices follow. The control sits in a collapsed Maintenance disclosure at the end of the record it affects, never as a window or a top-level button, and it opens itself while a flow is past idle so a retained receipt is never hidden. It is offered only when the plan, apply and receipt operations are all on the live WebSocket selection, and never remotely. Selections freeze while a flow is past idle, and each flow has one recovery slot per subject and action. Copy says what is cleared and what stays, and uses "Clear", "Redact" and "Tombstone", never "delete" or "complete".

## Consequences

Owners without shipped maintenance keep their existing domain controls, such as Brain deletion, Browse profiles, Roles edits, Auth removal and Access revocation. Device-local state still needs its own client contract.
